About Us

Empowering rural Ghana through sustainable development

PureEden Foundation (PEF) is a community-based non-profit organisation in Ghana, committed to enhancing the wellbeing of rural communities through sustainable development initiatives and humanitarian programs.

Established in 2023, PEF has been addressing the interconnected social, environmental, and economic challenges that perpetuate extreme poverty in rural Ghanaian communities — providing essential educational resources to basic schools, empowering marginalised communities with vital skills, and equipping women to enhance their economic independence and self-sufficiency.

Our Mission

A brighter future for all

To break the cycle of poverty in rural Ghana by fostering sustainable development — empowering communities with the knowledge, resources, and opportunities they need to create lasting change.

Our Vision

A movement for equity

To grow into a powerful social movement equipped to champion policy reforms that improve marginalised communities’ access to education, economic opportunity, and overall wellbeing.

What We Do

Focused on lasting change

Our initiatives tackle the social, economic, and environmental roots of poverty — improving access to the essential services every community deserves.
